About the game

Över Göteborg is a free browser game where you walk around the heart of Gothenburg: from Centralen and Drottningtorget past Brunnsparken, Kungsportsplatsen and the cathedral to Grönsakstorget, Stenpiren and Järntorget. The houses and streets come from the map, and the trams run every line through the area on Västtrafik's timetable, in Gothenburg's new 45 metre long cars.

Things to do

  • Step aboard a tram at a stop and ride it round the curves. It calls out the next stop.
  • Read the departure board on the platform and watch the tram roll in when the board says Nu.
  • Find Kopparmärra, the copper horse at Kungsportsplatsen, and listen for the cathedral's bell on the hour.

No account and nothing to install: the game runs in the browser on computers, phones and tablets. Add it to your home screen and it starts full screen.

More about the game

The trams

The timetable is Västtrafik's own, for a weekday, a Saturday and a Sunday, and public holidays run as Sundays. Every tram is where the clock puts it, so everyone who plays meets the same trams. No tram runs into another: where two would meet at a junction, one waits at the stop before, and a tram that turns at Drottningtorget goes on as its next trip or rolls out of town signed Ej i trafik, not in service.

Gothenburg right now

The game's clock is Gothenburg's clock, and the weather is the real weather. When it rains in town it rains in the game, and when SMHI issues a warning for Västra Götaland the game shows it too. In the evening the windows and the trams' lamps light up, gulls cry over the canals by day and the cathedral strikes the hours.

Under Stockholm

Över Göteborg is built on Joel Hägvall's game Under Stockholm, a stylized Stockholm metro in the browser, and shares its engine, its light and its quality checks.
